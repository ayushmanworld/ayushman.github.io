OUR STORY

Born from love,
for Ayushman

"Ayushman — whose name means 'blessed with long life' — sees the world in vivid, beautiful ways that others often miss."

His journey with autism inspired his family to build something bigger: a global movement ensuring no child ever feels unseen. What began as a father's love letter to his son has become a worldwide community united by one belief — every child deserves to shine.

We know firsthand the weight of therapy costs, school rejections, and isolation. Ayushman exists so no family carries that weight alone — whether you're in Bangalore, Mumbai, New York, or London.

Evidence-Based Best Practices for Parents

Proven strategies endorsed by global autism organisations. Practical, actionable, and free to implement.

🗣️

AAC — Augmentative & Alternative Communication

PECS, sign language and speech-generating devices are evidence-based for non-verbal children. Start early — even at 12–18 months for best results.

Learn more →
🎯

ABA — Applied Behaviour Analysis

The most researched autism intervention globally. Break tasks into small steps, use positive reinforcement. BCBA-supervised ABA shows strongest evidence for skill development.

Learn more →
🧘

Sensory Diet Planning

A personalised daily schedule of sensory activities — proprioception, vestibular and tactile input — reduces meltdowns and improves focus. Work with an OT to design your child's plan.

Learn more →
📅

Visual Schedules & Predictability

Autistic children thrive on routine. Visual schedules (picture cards or apps) reduce anxiety, improve transitions and build independence. Free templates from Autism Speaks.

Learn more →
🏃

Physical Exercise as Therapy

20 minutes of aerobic exercise before school improves attention and reduces stimming in children with autism. Swimming and martial arts show particular benefit in studies.

View research →
🍎

Nutrition & Gut Health

Many autistic children have gut sensitivities. Omega-3 supplementation shows positive effects on attention. Consult a paediatric dietitian before major dietary changes.

Learn more →
👨‍👩‍👧

Parent-Mediated Intervention (PMI)

Parents trained in naturalistic developmental behavioural interventions (NDBIs) show outstanding outcomes. ESDM and PRT are gold-standard PMI programs — free guides available.

Learn more →
🏫

IEP — Individualised Education Plan

Every child with autism has a legal right to an IEP in India (RPwD Act 2016). Know what to request, how to advocate effectively and what schools are legally obligated to provide.

IEP Guide →
🇮🇳

India-specific: Rights under RPwD Act 2016

The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act guarantees free education, reservation in government jobs, and social benefits for your child. Know your rights →
