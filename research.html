My child was just diagnosed with autism
First steps after receiving an ASD diagnosis — what to do in the next 30 days.
▼ See what to do

Immediate Steps (First 30 Days)

  • Don't panic — early intervention changes outcomes dramatically. Start within weeks, not months.
  • Request a detailed assessment from a developmental paediatrician at NIMHANS or Fortis.
  • Download the Autism Speaks 100-Day Kit — free, available in Hindi.
  • Contact your nearest therapy centre for speech and OT evaluation (use our finder above).
  • Inform your child's school — request an IEP meeting under RPwD Act 2016.
  • Join a local parent support group — ask Ayushman to connect you (+91 82800 56665).
🏫
Schools are refusing to admit my child
Your child has a legal right to inclusive education. Here's how to enforce it.
▼ See what to do

Your Legal Rights

  • Under RPwD Act 2016, all government schools MUST admit children with disabilities. Private schools must make reasonable accommodations.
  • Send a formal written request to the Principal citing Section 16 of RPwD Act 2016.
  • If refused, file a complaint with the District Education Officer (DEO) of your area.
  • Contact Action For Autism — they provide free legal support for school admission cases.
  • Consider special schools (Tamana, Sethu) while mainstreaming advocacy continues.
  • Request a shadow teacher/aide through your school — Ayushman can help fund this.

Therapy is too expensive — I can't afford it
Options and subsidies available for families who cannot afford full-price therapy.
▼ See what to do

Financial Support Options

  • Apply for the ADIP Scheme (Assistance to Disabled Persons) — free assistive devices and some therapy funding from Govt of India.
  • Contact NIMHANS outpatient — govt-subsidised rates for speech/OT therapy.
  • Apply to Ayushman NGO for therapy subsidy — we cover up to 12 sessions/month for qualifying families.
  • Check your state's disability welfare board — most states have free therapy camps.
  • Request a sliding scale fee from private therapists — many offer 20–40% reduction for low-income families.
  • Explore home-based therapy training — trained parents can deliver 60% of ABA at home.

My child is non-verbal — what therapy helps?
Evidence-based communication therapies for minimally verbal or non-verbal children.
▼ See what to do

Communication Therapies

  • AAC (Augmentative & Alternative Communication) — PECS picture cards or Proloquo2Go app. Start immediately, even at age 2.
  • Speech-Language Therapy (SLT) — minimum 3x/week. Look for therapists trained in PROMPT or Hanen method.
  • Floortime / DIR — parent-led play-based therapy that builds communication through relationship. Free training available online.
  • Avoid waiting for "readiness" — the brain is most plastic before age 5. Start now.
  • Use visual supports at home — label everything with pictures + words.

My child has severe meltdowns and aggression
Understanding triggers and evidence-based de-escalation strategies.
▼ See what to do

Managing Meltdowns

  • Meltdowns are NOT tantrums — they are neurological overload. Never punish during a meltdown.
  • Identify triggers using an ABC chart (Antecedent-Behaviour-Consequence) — track for 2 weeks.
  • Create a sensory calm-down kit: noise-cancelling headphones, weighted blanket, fidget tools.
  • Consult an OT for a sensory diet — a personalised daily activity plan that prevents overload.
  • If aggression is escalating, consult a child psychiatrist at NIMHANS (not for medication first, but for assessment).
  • NEVER restrain physically except for safety — it escalates trauma responses.

I want my child to do sports / physical activity
Sports programs proven to benefit autistic children — and how to find them.
▼ See what to do

Sports & Physical Programs

  • Swimming — #1 recommended sport for autism. Improves motor control, body awareness, anxiety. Look for Halliwick method pools.
  • Martial Arts (especially Taekwondo / Karate) — builds focus, self-regulation and confidence. Many academies now have autism-friendly classes.
  • Equine therapy (horse riding) — strong evidence base. Search for therapeutic riding centres in your city.
  • Yoga and gymnastics — excellent for proprioception and calming the nervous system.
  • Use our finder above to locate sports academies with autism experience near you.
  • Special Olympics India — free participation for children with intellectual disabilities. Register here.

What government schemes can my child access?
Central and state government benefits, disability certificates and entitlements.
▼ See what to do

Key Government Schemes

  • Disability Certificate (UDID) — get this first. Needed for all schemes. Apply at your district hospital.
  • ADIP Scheme — free assistive devices including AAC devices, hearing aids, sensory tools.
  • National Trust Schemes — Niramaya (health insurance), Gharaunda (care homes), Samarth (skill development).
  • Income Tax Deduction — Section 80DD allows ₹75,000–1,25,000 deduction for parents of disabled children.
  • Free education — RPwD Act mandates free and appropriate education up to age 18 in all govt schools.
  • disabilityaffairs.gov.in — official portal for all central schemes.

  • Read: "The Reason I Jump" by Naoki Higashida — shifts perspective and reduces caregiver grief.

  • Remember: your child's best therapy is a regulated, rested parent. Your wellbeing IS their therapy.

Planning for my child's future (18+)
Long-term planning, adult services, employment and residential care options.
▼ See what to do

Future Planning

  • Legal guardianship — after age 18, you must apply for legal guardianship under the Mental Health Care Act 2017 to continue making decisions.
  • National Trust — Gharaunda scheme for adult residential care with dignity.
  • Samarth scheme — vocational skill training for adults with autism funded by National Trust.
  • Will and trust planning — set up a special needs trust to ensure lifetime financial security.
  • Supported employment — many companies (SAP, Microsoft, Infosys) have dedicated autism hiring programs.
  • Start transition planning at age 14 — don't wait until 18. Contact Ayushman for guidance.
