Evidence-Based Best Practices

Proven strategies endorsed by global autism organisations. Practical and free to implement.

🗣️

AAC — Augmentative & Alternative Communication

PECS, sign language and speech-generating devices are evidence-based for non-verbal children. Start early — even at 12–18 months for best results.

Learn more →
🎯

ABA — Applied Behaviour Analysis

Most researched autism intervention globally. BCBA-supervised ABA shows strongest evidence for skill development and adaptive behaviour improvement.

Learn more →
🧘

Sensory Diet Planning

A personalised daily schedule of sensory activities reduces meltdowns and improves focus. Work with an OT to design your child's plan.

Learn more →
📅

Visual Schedules & Predictability

Visual schedules reduce anxiety, improve transitions and build independence. Free templates from Autism Speaks for immediate home use.

Learn more →
🏃

Physical Exercise as Therapy

20 minutes of aerobic exercise before school improves attention and reduces stimming. Swimming and martial arts show particular benefit in studies.

View research →
🏫

IEP — Individualised Education Plan

Every child with autism has a legal right to an IEP in India under RPwD Act 2016. Know what to request and how to advocate effectively at school.

IEP Guide →
🇮🇳

India: Rights under RPwD Act 2016

The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act guarantees free education, therapy and social benefits. Know your rights →
